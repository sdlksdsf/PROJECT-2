# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Kimmino is a project for anyone who wants a browser that respects them. Participation is open regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people.
- Assume competence and good faith; ask before concluding malice.
- Accept review feedback on your work without treating it as a verdict on you.
- Keep discussions about the software. Users choose a browser, not a movement.
- Respect privacy: do not post other people's personal data, logs or screenshots containing it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, sexualised attention, or discriminatory remarks.
- Personal or political attacks, sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Dismissing a security or privacy report because it is inconvenient.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages), in project chat spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **conduct@kimmino.app**. Reports are read by the maintainers, kept confidential, and answered within seven days.

Responses scale with severity and history: a private clarification, a public correction, a temporary ban from interaction, or a permanent ban from the project. Maintainers who violate this document face the same process, handled by another maintainer where one is available.
